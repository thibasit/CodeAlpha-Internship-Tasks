Place your own video here as:
    input.mp4

Any content works - people, cars, buses, bicycles, dogs, etc.
A short 10-30 second clip is recommended for fast iteration on CPU.

sample_test_video.avi (included in this folder) is an OpenCV public-domain
sample pedestrian video, provided only so you can test the full pipeline
immediately. It is NOT a substitute for your own input.mp4 - use your own
footage for your actual submission.
